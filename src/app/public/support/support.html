Quick answers

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We'll send you a link to pick a new password. It works once, for 30 minutes.

Reset my password
How will I know my order is ready?

Keep your order page open. It updates live, and the moment the kitchen marks your order Ready, it beeps and turns Tomato.

The cashier can't scan my code.

Turn your screen brightness up and tap your code to open it full screen. If it still won't scan, the cashier can type in the code shown under it.

Where are my points?

Your balance sits on your home screen next to your code. You get 15 points for every order you pick up.

I run a restaurant. How do I join?

We set up restaurant accounts for you. Write to us and tell us about your place.
