FAQ

Good questions.

How does the restaurant know it's me?

Every BeepBite account has its own QR code. The cashier scans it, and your order is added to your account.

How will I know my order is ready?

Keep your order page open. It updates live, and the moment the kitchen marks your order Ready, it beeps and turns Tomato.

Where do I see my points and old orders?

Your points balance sits on your home screen next to your code. Every order you've picked up is in your order history.

Does it cost anything to sign up?

No. Create an account with your name, email and a password, and your code is ready straight away.
